THE RESEARCH / SOURCE NOTES

What the map measures.

01 / RELIGION

Congregations, not buildings

“Congregations” refers to congregations reported by participating religious groups in the 2020 U.S. Religion Census, not just Christian churches. The figures cover multiple faith traditions and do not represent a complete directory of church buildings. Some groups do not report.

02 / SCHOOLS

Official K–12 school locations

NCES Common Core of Data 2024–25 public schools and Private School Survey 2023–24 private schools. Historical directories can include schools whose operational status has since changed.

03 / LOCAL DIRECTORY

Records are not interchangeable

School names and addresses come from NCES. Worship listings, when available, come from DHS/HIFLD's geocoded nonprofit inventory and may be incomplete or outdated. They are not used to calculate the congregation map.

DHS HIFLD ↗
04 / GEOGRAPHY

Rates, ratios and county boundaries

Population rates divide each count by 2020 population, then multiply by 10,000. The two ratios are (public + private schools) ÷ reported congregations × 100 and reported congregations ÷ (public + private schools) × 100. Population normalization does not apply to either, so the rate control is disabled for both. National and filtered ratios use pooled totals, not averages of county ratios. Small denominators can produce extreme values; a zero denominator or missing count makes a county's ratio undefined. Boundaries use US Atlas Census-derived geography. Historical FIPS changes can cause unmatched counties.

US Atlas geography ↗

Coverage: the 50 states and District of Columbia in the map. Alaska and Hawaii are included. U.S. territories are not visualized here. Missing reports are shown as missing—not as zero. Counts are not a guarantee of every currently active location. No Google Places data are harvested or stored.
