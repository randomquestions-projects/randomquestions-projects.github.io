About the geographic scope

Flow records are estimated or suppressed according to their source rules. IRS returns are not a person-level census of movers. County migration is different from a city's net migration. Rankings reflect the available published geographic coverage.

Employment and affordability are not interchangeable.

County workplace jobs do not measure resident employment within any individual city. National price parity compares price levels, not cost changes over time. Census contract rent ÷ income is only an affordability indicator, not a full cost-of-living index.

What we do not yet know

School quality (test results, graduation, teacher ratios), school-district eligibility, road travel time, mountains/beach drive times, state parks and climate hazards still require separate verified datasets. Missing provider data remains blank—not zero.
