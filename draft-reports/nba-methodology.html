How we evaluate NFL and NBA draft picks.

Two different questions need two different scores: how good was the player's NBA career, and how much value did the team receive relative to the overall pick?

Historical coverage

The NBA dataset contains 1,602 draft selections from 2000 through 2026, both rounds, including players who never appeared in an NBA regular-season game. Career totals and advanced impact data come from a 2000–2025 NBA draft history aggregation incorporating NBA Stats and Basketball-Reference-derived records. Career information is currently complete through the 2025–26 NBA season, with 2026–27 not yet finalized.

Original draft team refers to the selecting team; a draft-night trade may transfer player rights to another franchise. Career statistics include points, rebounds, assists, games, minutes, steals, blocks, per-game averages, career Win Shares, Win Shares per 48, VORP and BPM. Missing advanced impact statistics are not evidence of a zero-value career.

Career quality (0–100)

Draft-position-independent scores use midrank percentiles against the 2000–2017 reference population. Missing metrics are omitted and weights renormalized; confirmed nonparticipants score zero.

Career pace (0–100)

Exact first-k-completed-season comparisons with historical 2000–2017 draftees. NFL uses position production, top-three seasonal production and dated honors; NBA uses production, minutes, top-three seasonal production and dated honors. No annualized full-career comparison, no incomplete season, and no fabricated historical AV. At least 30 matched peers are required; missing comparable data results in no pace score. Undated Pro Bowls and All-Star totals are excluded from pace.

Draft value and grade

Expected quality is estimated from historical draft slots with smoothed, nonincreasing pick expectations. Observed quality equal to expected quality earns 50 (C). Above expectation, scores rise in proportion to remaining possible upside; below expectation they fall in proportion to expected return delivered. Both sports use the same grading thresholds. Three or four completed seasons: positive provisional grades only if pace beats its stage-specific pick expectation and value reaches 65. At five completed seasons and beyond, grades are established but dynamic. Recent classes have independent quality and pace where available without premature grades.

Accolades and known limitations

  • MVP, Defensive Player of the Year, Finals MVP and Rookie of the Year: winner seasons 2000–2026. These are NBA annual award winner ledgers, not all vote recipients.
  • All-NBA: first, second and third team selections, 2000–2026, matched by player and draft chronology. Tied or name-ambiguous matches remain unassigned rather than guessed.
  • All-Defensive: first- and second-team selections are recorded from 2000–2026, including tie years, based on the NBA's official historic honor roll and contemporary season-level selections. Award names that cannot be unambiguously tied to a player draft record remain unassigned.
  • All-Star: available career selection counts from a Wikipedia-derived history; the dataset does not provide a verified per-season All-Star list and therefore no seasons are invented.
  • NBA titles: the displayed count is a regular-season appearance for a team that ultimately won the championship that season, NOT an independent count of championship rings, Finals appearances or postseason roster eligibility.
  • 2026 draft: selection records and prospect positions are included. The 2026–27 NBA season is in progress, so these rookies are ungraded.

Backtest and data quality

Both candidate models beat the constant-mean benchmark on the 2018–2020 retrospective holdout. This is not predictive validation. Career quality may include partial NFL career totals from the original snapshot; exact-stage pace excludes 2026. The Hall of Fame tiers are descriptive quality-based watchlists, not calibrated induction probabilities. Source tables, checksums and scoring code remain private.
