DRAFT GRADE A+ THROUGH F / NFL V3 · NBA V2

NFL v3 evaluates career production, playing time, and major individual achievements. Career Weighted Approximate Value carries the greatest weight; MVP, Offensive/Defensive Player of the Year, AP first- and second-team All-Pro, Pro Bowl selections, and championship-team rosters contribute bounded credit. Championships carry deliberately less influence because they are team accomplishments. Draft value is calibrated to historical outcomes for original pick ranges with career maturity protections for recent classes. The previous NFL v2 model achieved retrospective holdout RMSE 16.63 versus 20.50 for a constant baseline; this is not a validation of v3. This is not a forecast. NBA v2 uses career Win Shares, VORP, BPM, minutes and bounded honors, with full 2000–2026 All-Defensive season selections. The previous NBA v1 retrospective holdout achieved RMSE 18.58 versus 20.89 for a constant baseline; this is not a validation of v2. Both backtests are retrospective, not future forecasts. The raw source tables and scoring code remain private.
