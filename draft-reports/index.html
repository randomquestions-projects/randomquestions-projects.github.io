DRAFT GRADE A+ THROUGH F / NFL V2 · NBA V1

NFL v2 evaluates career production, playing time, and major individual achievements. Career Weighted Approximate Value carries the greatest weight; MVP, Offensive/Defensive Player of the Year, AP first- and second-team All-Pro, Pro Bowl selections, and championship-team rosters contribute bounded credit. Championships carry deliberately less influence because they are team accomplishments. Draft value is calibrated to historical outcomes for original pick ranges with career maturity protections for recent classes. On the 2018–2020 retrospective holdout, the model achieved RMSE 16.63 against a 20.50 constant baseline. This is not a forecast. NBA v1 uses career Win Shares, VORP, BPM, minutes and bounded honors, with full 2000–2026 All-Defensive season selections. The 2018–2020 NBA draft holdout achieved an RMSE of 18.58 against 20.89 for a constant baseline. Both backtests are retrospective, not future forecasts. The raw source tables and scoring code remain private.
