DRAFT GRADE A+ THROUGH F / NFL V4 · NBA V3

NFL v4 reduces overlap between value measures and adds position-specific production and peak performance. NBA v3 adds peak production and postseason contribution. Both models use midrank percentiles, smooth uncapped-input honors credit, exact completed-season career-stage comparisons, and a shared pick-value scale where meeting expectations earns a C. NFL pace is unavailable for positions without comparable historical seasonal data. Career quality remains a descriptive snapshot, not a forecast, and includes available career totals.
